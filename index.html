Learn⏱ 4 min

What is data?

Data is a collection of facts and figures – words, numbers, pictures or sounds – that can be stored and used by a computer.

💡 Data on its own is raw. When we organise it and it starts to make sense, it becomes information.
Data: 32, 34, 36  →  Information: "Sharjah got hotter from Monday to Wednesday."

The 4 types of data

🔢Numbers

Values we can count, measure or calculate.

  • Age: 11
  • Marks: 87
  • Temperature: 34 °C

🔤Text

Letters, words and sentences.

  • Name: Aisha
  • Message: "Eid Mubarak!"
  • City: Sharjah

🖼️Images

Photos and drawings. A computer stores them as tiny dots called pixels.

8 × 8 = 64 pixels make this heart

🎵Sound

Voice, music and alerts. Stored as sound waves.

  • Voice note
  • School bell
  • Song

AI Connect⏱ 2 min

How does AI use data? 🤖

AI is like a student – it learns from examples. The more good, organised data it gets, the smarter it becomes.

📥 Collect data➜🗂️ Organise & label➜🧠 AI learns patterns➜🎯 AI predicts
🖼️
Images – Face unlock learned from thousands of photos of faces.
🎵
Sound – Voice assistants learned from millions of voice recordings.
🔤
Text – Translation apps learned from millions of Arabic and English sentences.
🔢
Numbers – Weather apps predict tomorrow's temperature from years of past readings.
🇦🇪 The UAE uses AI and data in smart services – e.g. Dubai's RTA uses traffic data to plan smoother roads, and the UAE has its own Minister of State for Artificial Intelligence.
